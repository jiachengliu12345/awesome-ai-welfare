# Shared glossary

[简体中文](GLOSSARY.zh-CN.md)

English and Chinese terms used in this collection, with definitions and supporting literature.

| English | 中文 | Definition | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| phenomenal consciousness | 现象意识 | Having subjective experience: there is something it is like for the subject. This is distinct from intelligence or successful access/reporting. | [Agnosticism about artificial consciousness](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/mila.70010), [Consciousness and the Moral Status of AI Agents](https://www.xsyk021.com/article/id/812242ec-3930-4034-8fda-68be071cc132) |
| sentience | 感受性 | In this welfare context, capacity for positively or negatively valenced experience. Some authors use the term more broadly. | [Can LLMs make trade-offs involving stipulated pain and pleasure states?](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.02432v1), [Agnosticism about artificial consciousness](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/mila.70010), [Consciousness and the Moral Status of AI Agents](https://www.xsyk021.com/article/id/812242ec-3930-4034-8fda-68be071cc132) |
| welfare / wellbeing | 福利／福祉 | How well or badly things go for a subject. Different theories emphasize experience, satisfied desires, or other goods. | [Taking AI Welfare Seriously](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00986v1), [Is there a tension between AI safety and AI welfare?](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11098-025-02302-2) |
| moral status / moral standing | 道德地位 | An entity matters morally for its own sake; arguments differ about what grounds that standing and how much weight it has. | [Taking AI Welfare Seriously](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00986v1), [Consciousness and the Moral Status of AI Agents](https://www.xsyk021.com/article/id/812242ec-3930-4034-8fda-68be071cc132) |
| moral patient | 道德受体 | An entity that can be affected by actions and whose own interests merit moral consideration. The Chinese equivalent here is an editorial convention, not an official translation. | [Taking AI Welfare Seriously](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00986v1), [Robot Rights? Let's Talk about Human Welfare Instead](https://arxiv.org/html/2001.05046v1) |
| moral patienthood | 道德受体地位 | The status of being a moral patient. Use moral patient for the entity and moral patienthood for this status. | [Taking AI Welfare Seriously](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00986v1) |
| moral agency | 道德能动性 | Capacity associated with moral judgment and responsibility; it is not the same question as being owed moral consideration. | [Taking AI Welfare Seriously](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00986v1), [Robot Rights? Let's Talk about Human Welfare Instead](https://arxiv.org/html/2001.05046v1) |
| robust agency | 稳健能动性 | A substantive ability to pursue goals through beliefs, desires, intentions, planning, and related capacities; Long et al. discuss it as a possible route to moral patienthood. | [Taking AI Welfare Seriously](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00986v1) |
| legal personhood | 法律人格 | An institutional legal status, distinct from a finding of consciousness or moral standing. This collection examines arguments, not jurisdiction-specific legal entitlements. | [Robot Rights? Let's Talk about Human Welfare Instead](https://arxiv.org/html/2001.05046v1), [Is there a tension between AI safety and AI welfare?](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11098-025-02302-2) |
| self-report | 自我报告 | An output statement about the system’s own states. Its relation to those states requires calibration rather than being assumed. | [Towards Evaluating AI Systems for Moral Status Using Self-Reports](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.08576v1), [Emergent Introspective Awareness in Large Language Models](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/introspection/index.html) |
| valence | 效价 | The positive or negative character of an experience. A numerical reward or negative-sounding output alone does not establish experiential valence. | [Can LLMs make trade-offs involving stipulated pain and pleasure states?](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.02432v1), [Claude 4 System Card — Opus 4 welfare assessment](https://assets.anthropic.com/m/6c940a1b69ed6a1c/original/Claude-4-System-Card.pdf) |
| functional introspection | 功能性内省 | Accurate, causally grounded reporting about some internal representations in the tested setting; broader philosophical significance remains open. | [Emergent Introspective Awareness in Large Language Models](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/introspection/index.html) |
| model | 模型 | The parameterized system and its configuration. A label does not identify every running execution or context. | [Towards Evaluating AI Systems for Moral Status Using Self-Reports](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.08576v1), [Claude 4 System Card — Opus 4 welfare assessment](https://assets.anthropic.com/m/6c940a1b69ed6a1c/original/Claude-4-System-Card.pdf) |
| running instance | 运行实例 | A particular execution with a particular context or state. Whether it forms a continuing welfare subject needs further argument. | [Towards Evaluating AI Systems for Moral Status Using Self-Reports](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.08576v1), [Taking AI Welfare Seriously](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00986v1) |
| persona | 对话角色 | A character or role expressed in conversation. Its apparent preferences should be distinguished from model-wide properties and other instances. | [Towards Evaluating AI Systems for Moral Status Using Self-Reports](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.08576v1), [Claude 4 System Card — Opus 4 welfare assessment](https://assets.anthropic.com/m/6c940a1b69ed6a1c/original/Claude-4-System-Card.pdf) |
