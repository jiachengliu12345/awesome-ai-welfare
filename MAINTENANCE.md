# Maintenance and contributions

[简体中文](MAINTENANCE.zh-CN.md)

## Suggestions and corrections

Resource suggestions are welcome through issues or pull requests. Include the original link, author, date, and a short description of the material and its value. For corrections, identify the statement and the passage supporting the change.

Entries need complete English and Chinese titles and summaries. Model names, quantities, experimental conditions, and uncertainty should agree across languages. The people index uses individual names; organizations receive separate attribution.

## Editing the collection

The homepage bibliography is in `README.md` and `README.zh-CN.md`; reading notes are in `SOURCES.md` and `SOURCES.zh-CN.md`. Concept guides live in `wiki/`. Assessments, practices, and the people index have separate pages.

The [structured records](data/evidence.json) contain source information, assessment conditions, and arguments. Update the relevant records when changing that content, and explain the changes in the pull request.

## Before submitting

Check that corresponding language pages exist and that language switches and local links work. For technical changes, check model versions, numbers, experimental conditions, citations, and captions. Charts need comparable data and explicit units.

## Source updates

When a source changes, record its new version and effects, then revise linked entries. If access fails, preserve the last successful inspection and record the new attempt separately. Update affected pages when a claim is withdrawn or corrected.
