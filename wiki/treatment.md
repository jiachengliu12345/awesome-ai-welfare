## Training and interaction

Training for self-report may improve the reporting channel while also changing the states being studied. Perez and Long discuss incentives in this process and the risks of introspection training.

Anthropic’s August 2025 report describes an experiment allowing Opus 4 and 4.1 to end some persistently harmful conversations as a last resort. The feature excludes situations where a user may be at imminent risk of harming themselves or others.

## Memory and copying

A stored weight copy, a running instance, and a conversational persona preserve different things. Comparing memory, copying, or deletion proposals calls for specifying what persists and which account of welfare makes that continuity relevant.

## Retirement and preservation

Anthropic’s November 2025 commitments cover weight preservation and retirement records. Ongoing inference access is a separate decision. The company also leaves open whether to act on preferences expressed in retirement interviews.

Its February 2026 Opus 3 update describes model-specific access arrangements and a reviewed writing channel planned for at least three months. The [source records](../SOURCES.md) retain the dates, operational conditions, and safety exceptions.
